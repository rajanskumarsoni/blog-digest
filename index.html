💡 Feed URL formats & hints
  • Common Suffixes: append /feed, /rss, /rss.xml, or /atom.xml to the blog address.
  • Substack: https://[newsletter].substack.com/feed
  • Medium: https://medium.com/feed/@[username] or https://medium.com/feed/[pub]
  • WordPress / Ghost: usually at https://[domain]/feed/
  • GitHub Releases: https://github.com/[owner]/[repo]/releases.atom
